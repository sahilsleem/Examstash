Notice & Takedown Procedure

If you are a copyright owner or an authorized representative and believe that any document, question paper, or link available on ExamStash infringes your intellectual property rights, please send a written notification containing the following details:

  • Identification: A description or title of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed.
  • Exact URL: The exact page URL(s) on ExamStash where the material is located.
  • Contact Information: Your name, organization (if applicable), and email address.
  • Good-Faith Statement: A statement that you have a good-faith belief that use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.

Submit a Notice

Please send all takedown notices or copyright inquiries to our verified contact channels. We aim to review valid requests promptly upon receipt and verification of the required information, and will remove or disable access to the relevant material accordingly.
