# Islamia College Portal (ICSC Srinagar) 🎓

Previous-year question papers, syllabus, and study resources for **Islamia College of Science & Commerce, Srinagar (Autonomous)**.

**Live site → [https://examstash.pages.dev/](https://examstash.pages.dev/)**

---

## 🌟 Overview

A fast, lightweight, and dedicated academic portal built specifically for students of **Islamia College of Science & Commerce (ICSC), Hawal, Srinagar**. 

Provides direct access to semester-wise question papers and official syllabi without any mandatory accounts, popups, or download walls.

---

## 📚 Degree Programs Covered

### Undergraduate Courses (Semesters 1 to 6)
* 💻 **BCA:** Bachelor of Computer Applications

* 💼 **BBA:** Bachelor of Business Administration
* 📊 **B.Com:** Bachelor of Commerce (General & Honours)
* 📖 **B.A:** Bachelor of Arts (Humanities & Social Sciences)

* ⚙️ **MCA:** Master of Computer Applications
* 📈 **M.Com:** Master of Commerce

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* 🔍 **Instant Search:** In-memory autocomplete search across all college degrees, subjects, and semesters (`Ctrl + K` or `/`).
* 👁️ **In-Browser PDF Previews:** View past exam papers directly without leaving the site.
* 🔖 **Saved Papers:** Bookmark important semester papers to browser storage for quick offline access.
* 📱 **Mobile PWA App:** Installable on Android & iOS with a sticky mobile bottom navigation bar.
* 📲 **Classroom Sharing:** 1-tap WhatsApp and Telegram buttons to share papers with college batch groups.
* 📩 **Request a Paper:** Submit requests or share new examination papers directly via Telegram.

## 💻 Local Development

```bash
git clone https://github.com/sahilsleem/Examstash.git
cd Examstash
python -m http.server 8000
```
Open **[http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000)** in your browser.
