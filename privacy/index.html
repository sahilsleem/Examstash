Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 10, 2026

At ExamStash (accessible from https://examstash.online/), we prioritize the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information that may be collected or processed when you use our educational website and how that information is handled.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

ExamStash is designed for free, friction-free student access. We do not require user accounts, logins, registrations, payment details, or personal identity submissions to browse or download past question papers and syllabi.

2. Server Log Data & Security

Like most websites, our hosting and content delivery network (Cloudflare) automatically records non-personally identifiable server log details when you visit our pages. This may include your IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, and access timestamps. This data is processed strictly for network security, DDoS protection, and site performance maintenance.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements on populated resource pages to support server upkeep and platform operations. Google, as a third-party advertising vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to display relevant advertisements to visitors based on their visits to ExamStash and other websites across the internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by adjusting their browser cookie preferences.

4. Third-Party Links & External Storage

ExamStash provides direct links to external services, including Google Drive (where academic PDF files are hosted) and social messaging platforms (Telegram and Instagram). When you navigate to an external link, your interaction is governed by the privacy policy and terms of that third-party provider.

5. Contacting Us

If you reach out to ExamStash via email (examstash1@gmail.com) or Telegram, any information you provide (such as your email address and message contents) will be used solely to respond to your inquiry or review your submitted study materials.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or regulatory changes. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
