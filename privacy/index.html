Privacy Policy

At ExamStash (accessible from https://examstash.online/), we prioritize the privacy and trust of our visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information that may be collected or processed when you browse our educational website and how that data is handled.

SECTION 01

1. Information We Do Not Collect

ExamStash is engineered for seamless student access. We do not require user accounts, logins, registrations, credit card or payment details, or personal identity submissions to browse, read, or download past question papers and syllabi.

SECTION 02

2. Server Log Data & Security

Like most websites, our hosting and content delivery network (Cloudflare) automatically records non-personally identifiable server log details when you visit our pages. This may include your IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, and access timestamps.

This data is processed strictly for network performance optimization, DDoS mitigation, caching, and infrastructure security.

SECTION 03

3. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements on populated resource pages to support server upkeep and platform operations. Google, as a third-party advertising vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to display relevant advertisements to visitors based on their visits to ExamStash and other websites across the internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by configuring their browser cookie settings.

SECTION 04

4. Third-Party Links & External Storage

ExamStash provides direct links to external services, including Google Drive (where academic PDF files are hosted) and social messaging platforms (Telegram and Instagram). When you navigate to an external link, your interaction is governed by the privacy policy and terms of that third-party provider.

SECTION 05

5. Contacting Us & Message Retention

If you reach out to ExamStash via email (examstash1@gmail.com) or Telegram, any information you provide (such as your email address and message contents) will be used solely to respond to your inquiry or review your submitted study materials.

SECTION 06

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect operational, architectural, or regulatory changes. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

✉️ Questions Regarding Privacy?

If you have any questions or require additional details regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at examstash1@gmail.com.
