Finding past exam papers shouldn't be the hardest part of exam preparation.

Semester revision requires reviewing real question patterns, understanding mark distributions, and consulting the official syllabus blueprints. ExamStash eliminates broken messaging links and lost PDFs by organizing verified academic materials in one fast, open repository.

01
Platform Mission

Built by students, for students.

ExamStash is an independent, free educational resource platform built to solve a simple, universal student frustration: finding reliable past semester papers when preparing for examinations at Islamia College of Science & Commerce (ICSC), Srinagar.

We believe study resources should be instantly available to everyone without friction, mandatory accounts, paywalls, or download restrictions.

What We Provide Core Features
⚡

Zero-Restriction Direct Downloads

Every single question paper and syllabus PDF is hosted on fast, verified Google Drive storage. You can view papers directly in your browser or download them for offline study without logging in or waiting on countdown timers.

📚 NEP 2020 Structured Hierarchy

Organized systematically across Major, Minor, Multidisciplinary (MDC), and Skill Enhancement (SEC) courses across Semesters 1 through 6.

🎯 Paired Syllabi & Question Papers

Official curriculum blueprints paired alongside previous year exams so you know exactly what topics are covered.

Academic Utility

Find it. Study it. Move on.

No mandatory logins. No advertising clutter blocking your documents. Just clean, rapid access to your semester study resources.
