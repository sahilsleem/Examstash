About ExamStash

Less searching. More studying.

Direct, free access to semester question papers and official syllabi for undergraduate degree programs at Islamia College of Science & Commerce, Srinagar.

Explore past papers →

Finding past exam papers shouldn't be the hardest part of exam preparation.

Semester revision requires reviewing real question patterns, understanding mark distributions, and consulting the official syllabus blueprints. ExamStash eliminates broken messaging links and lost PDFs by organizing verified academic materials in one fast, open repository.

Built by students, for students.

ExamStash is an independent, free educational resource platform built to solve a simple, universal student frustration: finding reliable past semester papers when preparing for examinations at Islamia College of Science & Commerce (ICSC), Srinagar.

We believe study resources should be instantly available to everyone without friction, mandatory accounts, paywalls, or download restrictions.

What we provideCore features

Zero-restriction direct downloadsEvery question paper and syllabus PDF is hosted on fast, verified Google Drive storage. View papers in your browser or download them for offline study without logging in or waiting on countdown timers.
NEP 2020 structured hierarchyOrganized across Major, Minor, Multidisciplinary (MDC), and Skill Enhancement (SEC) courses, Semesters 1 through 6.
Paired syllabi & question papersOfficial curriculum blueprints paired alongside previous year exams so you know exactly what topics are covered.
